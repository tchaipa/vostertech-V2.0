Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company registration take? +

Most registrations are completed within 48 hours of submitting all required documents. Complex structures may take slightly longer. We keep you informed throughout.

What documents do I need to register a company? +

You'll need national IDs for all directors, proof of address, your proposed company name, and your Memorandum of Association. We guide you through the full checklist.

Do you offer a free initial consultation? +

Yes! We offer a free 30-minute consultation to understand your needs and recommend the best path forward. No commitment required.

Can I register a company remotely? +

Absolutely. Our process is fully digital. You can submit all documents online and receive your certificate via email. No in-person visit required.

What types of business structures do you register? +

We register private limited companies (Pvt Ltd), public companies, sole traders, partnerships, trusts, non-profit organisations, and more. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

Our Story

Built to Help Businesses Succeed

VosterTech Consultancy was founded in 2012 with a simple mission: make business registration and compliance accessible, affordable, and stress-free for every entrepreneur in Zimbabwe and beyond.

Over the past 12 years, we've helped over 500 companies get off the ground — from solo traders to large corporations — and we continue to grow alongside our clients.

Today, we offer a full suite of business services under one roof, from registration and compliance to digital marketing and web development.
